Confirm you have the extracted SwimTracker-unsigned.ipa (done).
Install Apple's standalone iTunes + iCloud from apple.com (Windows only).
Install AltServer on the PC.
Plug in the iPhone over USB, trust the computer, and use AltServer to install AltStore onto the phone (sign in with your free Apple ID here).
In AltStore on the phone, install your SwimTracker-unsigned.ipa.
On the phone, Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID cert, then open the app.
Keep AltServer running on the PC so it can refresh over Wi-Fi before the 7-day clock runs out.
